Angus Neale is a London based data journalist and experienced television producer. Having worked on major event coverage for ITV News, he moved on to producing data stories for the ITV Peston programme. He is experienced in analysing polling and economic data and making stories accessible through strong visuals. You can find longer pieces on Substack and shorter takes on TikTok.
